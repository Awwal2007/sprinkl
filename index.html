Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sprinkl and how does it work?

Sprinkl is Nigeria's premier automated giveaway distribution platform. Hosts fund an in-app wallet, create a giveaway with a custom link, and share it. When winners submit their bank details or crypto wallet, Sprinkl instantly pays them automatically.

How does Sprinkl prevent double-claims?

Sprinkl enforces atomic unique compound indexes on destination bank accounts and crypto wallet addresses. A destination cannot be paid more than once for a giveaway.

What happens to unclaimed giveaway funds?

Leftover or unclaimed funds from expired campaigns are automatically refunded directly to your Sprinkl host wallet balance.
